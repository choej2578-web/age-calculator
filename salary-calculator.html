How This Salary Calculator Works

Annual salary = Hourly Rate × Hours per Week × Weeks per Year.

Monthly salary is estimated by dividing annual salary by 12.

Daily pay is estimated using a 5-day workweek.

This calculator estimates gross pay before taxes, deductions, bonuses, and benefits.
